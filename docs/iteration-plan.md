## Retrospective (end of iteration)
* **Keep** — what worked: Work was tracked on the GitHub Projects board, with issues linked to pull requests and the documents (D1, D2, D3, D5) merged early through PRs. All planned items, including the optional F7, were finished.
* **Problem** — what did not: This plan was only completed late, so the board and the plan were out of sync at delivery, and the feature work started late in the iteration. Business rules were placed in services instead of domain classes, money was stored as `double`, services threw raw `Exception`, and `ConsoleUI` grew into a 617-line god class. These were fixed after the Iteration 1 review: rules moved into `Cart`, `CartLine` and `MenuItem`, public setters removed, a `Money` type introduced, specific unchecked exceptions used, and `ConsoleUI` split into command handlers and formatters.
* **Try** — one concrete change for the next iteration: Complete this file in the first planning meeting of Iteration 2, with owners, reviewers and estimates, and update it at every stand-up.
