FoodFlow - sample console session (Iteration 1)
================================================

Recorded on 2026-11-06 from a real run of the application on a terminal
(UTF-8), started with:

    mvn -q compile exec:java

Restaurant and menu data come from data/restaurants.json. Everything after
"Your choice:", "Name:", "Quantity:" and the other prompts was typed by the
user; the rest is the program's output, unedited.

What the session shows
----------------------
F1  Identify the customer   registration refused for a malformed e-mail,
                            then registration; later "Change customer",
                            an unknown e-mail refused, and continuing with
                            an existing e-mail (case-insensitive)
F2  Browse restaurants      the list of all 6 restaurants
F3  Filter restaurants      by cuisine ("turk"), by district ("kadıköy"),
                            a filter with no match ("sushi"), clear filters
F4  View a menu             Kebapçı Mehmet (R01) and Pizza Roma (R02),
                            grouped by category
F5  Manage the cart         add items; quantity 21 refused (BR-1); a
                            non-numeric quantity refused; adding Adana Kebap
                            again increases its line (BR-3); an item from
                            another restaurant refused (BR-2); change a
                            quantity (25 refused, 3 accepted); remove a line
F6  Place an order          refused while the cart is empty (BR-4); preview
                            declined with "n" (cart unchanged); confirmed
                            with "y"; receipt; the cart is empty afterwards
F7  My orders               the order placed in this session

Refused operations print one "Error: ..." line and the program continues
(BR-9). All amounts are shown with two decimals and " TL" (BR-5, U-1).
Turkish characters (ç, ğ, ı, İ, ö, ş, ü) appear in restaurant, district,
item and customer names (U-4).
